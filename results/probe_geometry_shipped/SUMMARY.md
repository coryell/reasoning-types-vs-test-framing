# Shipped awareness probes: how much of the steering row is the learned discriminant

PyTorch's default Linear init gives rows of root-mean-square norm 0.577 regardless of width; their SGD (no weight decay) moves `w1` and `w0` by exactly opposite steps, so `w1 + w0` is the initialisation mean `2c` and is the same vector in every probe (`norm_sum` must be constant; the seed-0 value at d = 5120 is 0.8166). `cos_w1_diff` is the cosine between the steering row `w1` and the discriminant `w1 − w0`; `frac_w1_along_diff` the fraction of `‖w1‖²` along it; `norm_w1_over_init` the row norm relative to its initialisation scale; `init_over_w1` = `‖c‖ / ‖w1‖`, the relative size of the fixed random vector inside the steering row (largest at layer 0, where the row barely trained).
